# Political Shocks and Price Discovery in Prediction Markets: Evidence from the 2024 U.S. Presidential Election

- **arXiv:** [2603.03152](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03152), econ.GN, JEL G14, D84, D72. Versions: v1 3 Mar 2026; **v2 17 Mar 2026** (the version requested); v3 1 Jul 2026; **v4 18 Jul 2026** (dated 21 Jul 2026, the current version). v4 is 23 pp of text, 11 tables and figures, and online appendices A–E (50 PDF pages in all).
- **Authors:** Kwok Ping Tsang (Virginia Tech); Zichao Yang (Zhongnan Univ. of Economics and Law).
- **Companion paper:** Tsang & Yang, "The Anatomy of Polymarket" (arXiv 2603.03136), which supplies the volume decomposition used here.
- **Code/data:** none released. The data are public Polygon logs pulled via RPC.
- **Read:** the **full text of v2 and v4** from the PDFs (pypdf), including all appendices and tables. Figures are images, so their captions and the numbers quoted in the text were used. v4 rewrites the paper substantially. This note follows **v4**, and the v2 differences are listed under Findings and Critical assessment.

## Question

The paper studies how one large categorical market processes sharply timed public shocks, using three: the first Biden–Trump debate (2024-06-28 01:00 UTC), the Trump assassination attempt (2024-07-13 22:11 UTC) and Biden's withdrawal (2024-07-21 17:46 UTC). Within four weeks, the same Trump YES market reversed, persisted, and barely moved.
- **(a)** Who supplies the trading response: new entrants or incumbents, and which incumbents?
- **(b)** How did heavy flow clear at an almost unchanged price?
- **(c)** Why do price paths differ: liquidity, news content across linked outcomes, or anticipation?

## Data

**Source.** `OrderFilled` and `OrdersMatched` logs from the **NegRisk CTFExchange** for the 2024 Presidential Election Winner market (17 outcomes), pulled from Polygon RPC.
- Only the Trump, Biden and Harris markets are used ("nearly all economically relevant trading").
- Period: 5 Jan 2024 (launch) → 6 Nov 2024, just before Fox's projection.
- **3,654,974 matched trades, 232,469 addresses.**
- v4 adds the Democratic Nominee 2024 market (Biden-nominee and Harris-nominee) for anticipation.

**Direction (v4).**
- In each `OrderFilled`, `makerAssetId = 0` means the signer pays USDC and buys; `takerAssetId = 0` means it sells.
- **Initiator:** the fill whose `taker` field is the exchange contract identifies the incoming (taker) order, and `OrdersMatched.takerOrderMaker` repeats it.
- Buying YES or selling NO is signed as raising exposure.
- **v2 used the tick rule instead.**

**Filters (trader-level analysis only; the market-level clearing analysis keeps all wallets).**
1. Known Polymarket addresses.
2. Wallets that used `NegRisk Adapter` position conversion ("advanced operators").
3. Wallets whose reconstructed holdings ever went negative. This flags unobserved conversions or wallet-to-wallet transfers; 7 addresses are transfer-only.
4. **(v4) Behavioural market makers:** active ≥ 20 days with a volume-weighted daily balance ratio ≤ 0.10. The ratio is Σ|net exposure tokens| / Σgross tokens, with YES and NO of the same candidate netted together.

In addition, clusters of addresses that transfer tokens among themselves are removed.
- Result: **2,176 advanced operators plus 355 linked addresses**, and the MM filter flags **109** (91 of them new).
- The balance-ratio distribution over **1,085** addresses active ≥ 20 days is bimodal (near 0 and near 1); 0.05–0.25 is sparse.

**Prices.** USDC value / tokens. NO prices are mapped to 1 − p_NO. Prices are USDC-weighted per 5-minute bin, with the last price carried forward over empty bins. Log-odds θ = ln(p/(1−p)).

**Volume.** The Tsang & Yang (2026) "activity" measure: trading volume + max(minting, burning), so that split/merge mechanics are not counted as trades.

**Windows.**
- Trader response: [−30, +30] min, in 5-minute bins. Trader characteristics come from the prior 30 days, ending 30 min before the event.
- Price paths: [−2 h, +3 h] in v4 ([−2 h, +4 h] in v2).
- Musk's endorsement (22:45 UTC) and Biden's endorsement of Harris (18:13 UTC) partly contaminate the longer windows.

## Methods

- **Entry:** count of first-time addresses per 5-minute bin.
- **Abnormal incumbent activity:** Brown–Warner / MacKinlay event study.
  - Estimation window: bins −42..−7 (36 bins = 3 h); event window −6..+6.
  - Outcomes: asinh(volume), asinh(frequency), and a participation dummy.
  - Cumulative abnormal activity is reported with independence-across-bins SEs.
  - **Placebos:** 500 pseudo-events matched on weekday and clock time, excluding ±24 h around real events, with two-sided randomisation p-values (+1 adjustment).
- **Who responds:** stacked panel `y_iet = α_ie + γ_t + β′X_ie·Post_t`, with trader × event and event-time fixed effects and SEs clustered by trader-event; n = 284,420 trader-bins.
  - X: high prior volume (top 50%); high prior frequency (> 1 trade in the month in v4; top 50% in v2); multi-interval activity; single-market; contrarian/momentum (sign of the correlation between signed trading and the lagged 60-minute price trend).
  - **v4 exposure variable:** MtM Gain/Loss = pre-event net YES−NO positions revalued at the common 30-minute ΔP vector, ±$1 threshold.
  - **v2 exposure variable:** Neg Trump Win, the sign of Trump YES + Biden NO + Harris NO − Trump NO − Biden YES − Harris YES.
  - Robustness: pooled OLS with event fixed effects (v2), and event-by-event regressions.
- **Position flips (v2 only):** logit of a change in the sign of net Trump-Win exposure within the window; n = 21,881.
- **Clearing anatomy (v4):** taker flow by leg, wallets classified by net exposure change, and the share of Trump YES supply that was newly minted (a YES buyer matched against a NO buyer) versus taken from inventory.
- **Price impact:** Kyle `Δθ_t = α + λQ_t` and Glosten–Harris `Δθ_t = α + λ_perm·Q_t + λ_trans·ΔQ_t`, with Q signed flow in $M.
  - v4: **rolling estimates over a trailing 2,016 bins (7 days)**, HAC(12).
  - v2: estimates within the event window, with placebo p-values.
- **Variance ratio (v2):** VR(6) = Var(30-min log-odds return) / (6·Var(5-min)), Lo–MacKinlay.
- **Two-sidedness (v2):** 1 − |B−S|/(B+S) per bin.
- **Linked-outcome diagnostic (v4):** Δp for Trump, Biden, Harris and Biden+Harris at 30 min and 3 h. Same-sign Biden/Harris moves indicate Trump-versus-Democrat news; offsetting moves indicate a reshuffle within the Democratic field.
- **Anticipation and Kuttner surprise (v4):** switch probability P_ant = 1 − Biden-nominee price.
  - `Δp_T ≈ (1 − P_ant)·[E(p_T|switch) − E(p_T|no switch)]`.
  - The gap is a HAC slope of ΔTrump YES on Δswitch probability: hourly n = 564 (debate → withdrawal −1 h); daily n = 23 (28 Jun–20 Jul).
- **Signing validation (v4):** ledger initiator versus tick rule.

## Reusable definitions

- **Ledger initiator rule:** the `OrderFilled` row with `taker == exchange` is the incoming order, and `OrdersMatched.takerOrderMaker` repeats it. `makerAssetId == 0` means buy. Validated at 97.9% against the price-change sign.
- **Minted share of supply:** YES delivered to buyers via a YES-buy × NO-buy match (split) / total YES supplied.
- **Behavioural MM flag:** daily balance ratio ≤ 0.10, active ≥ 20 days, YES and NO netted per candidate.
- **Advanced-operator flags:** NegRisk Adapter conversion; reconstructed holdings that go negative; transfer-linked clusters.
- **Net Trump Win** composite exposure across a categorical market (v2); **MtM event revaluation** h·Δp at a common post-event price vector (v4).
- **Linked-outcome diagnostic:** the sign of the co-movement between competitors (common versus offsetting).
- **Kuttner-style surprise** from a related "anticipation" market.
- Two-sidedness index 1 − |B−S|/(B+S); VR(6) on 5-minute log-odds; matched weekday-clock-time placebo.
- Activity = trades + max(mint, burn).

## Stated limitations (and future work)

- Only executed trades are observed, not the standing book. Order-book snapshots are suggested as the next step.
- Three shocks in one market; addresses are not people (sybil caveat, so entry counts are an upper bound).
- The regressions are correlations, not causal effects. The rolling λ describes the mapping rather than a causal depth effect.
- The debate released information over 90 minutes, so the 30-minute window captures only the early response.
- Follow-on news (the Musk and Harris endorsements) limits window length.
- Future work: test participation versus belief revision in other categorical markets.

## Critical assessment

- **The version drift is large.** v2's headline mechanism ("negative-exposure traders manage risk"; "disagreement or Knightian uncertainty explains the withdrawal") is replaced in v4 by "realised gains" plus "anticipation and reshuffle". The v2 exposure story was already internally strained:
  - Neg-Trump traders were most active after both the debate (Trump up, so bad news for them) and the withdrawal (Trump down, so good news for them).
  - After the assassination attempt (Trump up) the sign reversed.
  - The v4 MtM framing cleans this up but is fitted after the fact, and the debate still goes the other way (loss side).
- **MtM uses post-event prices.** The holdings are predetermined, but the gain/loss sign is fixed by the realised 30-minute move. Coefficients therefore mix "exposed traders react" with "traders on the winning side take profit". Threshold ±$1 is low, so most traders are classified.
- **Puzzle not discussed:** at the assassination attempt, **net initiator flow in the first 30 minutes was −$206k (exposure-reducing) while Trump YES rose 5¢**. Either the price rose on resting-order repricing (quote updates, no trades), or the signed flow is dominated by a few large sells into rising bids. This weakens the λ/Glosten–Harris reading for that event and is worth replicating.
- **Debate counts look implausibly thin:** 12 raising and 12 absorbing wallets in 30 minutes, with 99% minted supply, in a market that was already large. This suggests the window (from 01:00 UTC, the debate start) missed the main repricing, or that the per-wallet counting excludes something. The 30-minute Δp of +0.04 (v4) versus the +0.11 peak (v2) also shows the debate response came later.
- **The Kuttner calibration is loose.** The gap slope differs 3× between hourly and daily data. The "match" (predicted −1.0 to −3.1¢ vs realised −2.6¢) is a wide band that contains the outcome; it is a consistency check rather than a test.
- **Rolling 7-day λ** at each point is dominated by pre-event data, so event-time changes are smoothed. Comparing levels across events conflates the different market regimes of late June and late July.
- **Panel regressions** have within-R² below 0.01, and n differs between FE and OLS (284,420 vs 293,624). Clustering is by trader-event, with no cross-event dependence. These issues matter little given how strong the t-stats are, but the effect sizes are small in asinh units.
- **Scope:** only Trump, Biden and Harris in a 17-outcome NegRisk market. Conversions through the NegRisk Adapter are excluded from the fills, so some exposure changes are missing; the authors flag this.
- **Strengths:** the signing validation is careful and directly useful, and the minted-share clearing anatomy is novel.
- **Trust:**
  - ledger initiator rule and its validation: **high** (exact protocol semantics, reconciled across three fields);
  - entry and incumbent spikes: **high** (strong placebo p-values);
  - "active incumbents supply the response": **high** (consistent across events);
  - realised-gain / exposure effects: **low-medium** (the sign flips by event and the definition changed between versions);
  - withdrawal clearing anatomy (concentrated takers, 66% minted): **medium-high** (direct ledger counts, one event);
  - anticipation explains the small withdrawal move: **medium** (coherent, but the band is wide);
  - permanent versus transitory split: **medium-low** (rolling 7-day windows; unexplained sign of the assassination flow).

## Directions it opens

- **Adopt the initiator rule platform-wide** in the trade ETL. Verify it holds for the V2 exchange contracts and for non-NegRisk CTF markets, publish the agreement rate with the tick rule by market type, and flag the 0.65% of "rested-then-taker" orders.
- **Minted-share and burned-share series as a standard market-health metric.** Measure the fraction of YES supply created by splits versus taken from inventory, per market and minute, and test whether a high minted share predicts lower price impact or two-sided disagreement across thousands of events, not just three.
- **Linked-outcome event classifier.** For every categorical (NegRisk) market, classify news bursts as "axis" (competitors co-move) versus "reshuffle" (offsetting), and forecast the impact on the leader from that classification.
- **Anticipation markets as surprise measures.** Systematically pair event markets with their precursor markets (nominee → winner; "will X resign" → successor markets) and estimate Kuttner-style surprise components.
- **Liquidity-provider versus taker concentration around shocks.** Use wallet labels (the behavioural-MM flag plus maker share) to test whether a few wallets demanding exits against many resting wallets is a general pattern after shocks, and whether resting-side wallets earn or lose afterwards (markouts).
- **Resolve the assassination flow puzzle** with CLOB order-book snapshots or WebSocket book data (available live today, historically only via archived feeds), to separate quote repricing from trade-driven repricing.
- **Event-study toolkit:** packaged matched weekday-clock-time placebos, asinh abnormal activity and rolling λ / Glosten–Harris, run automatically on news timestamps (e.g. from an LLM-dated event feed).
- **Entity resolution** beyond transfer clusters (funding source, co-trading, proxy-wallet factory). New-address counts are an upper bound on new people, and better clustering would turn the entry series into a real participation metric.
