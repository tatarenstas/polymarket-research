# OpenMarket: A Synchronized Polymarket–Binance Dataset for High-Frequency Prediction-Market Research

- **arXiv:** [2607.26245](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26245), v1 28 Jul 2026 (only version; PDF dated
  30 Jul 2026), q-fin.TR. 23 pp, 7 figures, 3 tables.
- **Author:** Gregory Young (Dept. of Computer Science, University of Colorado Boulder).
  Independent release, no funding, no competing interests declared.
- **Code/data:** github.com/gregyoung14/openmarket, tag **v0.5.2**, Apache 2.0 (Rust
  workspace, scripts, mdBook docs, quickstart notebook, Docker, sanitised operational
  archive). HF dataset `gregyoung14/openmarket-btc-polymarket` (splits
  `v0.4.3-unified`, `v0.2-full`, `v0.1-sample`); HF models `gregyoung14/openmarket-models`
  (`v0.2.1/`, `v0.1/`). No DOI yet. Collection ended; archive frozen 2026-07-01.
- **Read:** full text (PDF, all 23 pages; arXiv HTML cross-checked). Figures read from
  captions and embedded values. I did not inspect the repository or the dataset itself.

## Question

Originally: can Polymarket's BTC 15-minute Up/Down markets be traded profitably against
Binance BTC/USDT order flow? It could not. The paper therefore releases the data and
tooling, and reports five narrow claims:
1. top-of-book spreads are usually one tick;
2. source-clock lead–lag has a compact median and heavy tails;
3. Polymarket quotes follow large Binance moves by hundreds of ms (collector clock);
4. lead–lag barely changes across disagreement regimes;
5. a multivariate model slightly underperforms the Polymarket mid out-of-sample.

## Data

**Sources.** Binance BTC/USDT trades (trade ID, source ts, price, qty, quote volume,
maker/taker flag, ingest ts) and derived candles (1 s – 1 h). Polymarket market WebSocket
for BTC binaries: book updates, trades and last-trade-price events; token IDs mapped to
slug and UP/DOWN side via market metadata. Single collector host; every event keeps
`source_ts_ms` and `ingest_ts_ms`.

**Period and coverage.** Event data 2026-02-12 → 2026-05-15 (93 calendar days), with data
on **54 Polymarket days** and 57 Binance days; multi-day gaps. 202 SQLite snapshots
published 2026-03-14 → 2026-07-01 (46.21 GB compressed); snapshots after 2026-05-15 add no
events.

**Unified split v0.4.3 (727,098,247 rows, 8.69 GiB).**

| Table | Rows |
| --- | ---: |
| polymarket_ticks_ms | 605.6M |
| binance_trades | 62.3M |
| binance_ticks_ms | 55.8M |
| lag_pairs_ms | 2,936,031 |
| market_meta | ~4K (4,450 markets) |
| candle tables | 498,525 |

Built by merging overlapping snapshot exports: 916M → 727M rows (~21% duplicates removed)
with table-specific dedupe keys. Five partial snapshots were recovered with
`sqlite3 .recover`; all 202 are "published-clean". The HF default viewer shows a larger
row count because it includes non-unified artefacts.

**Lead–lag pairs.** For each Polymarket tick, the nearest Binance tick by source time
within W = ±750 ms (ties by Binance ts, then row id); one-to-one on the Polymarket side,
many-to-one on Binance. lead_lag_ms = t_P − t_B. Stored with a quality flag (tight
≤ 100 ms, medium ≤ 300 ms, wide otherwise), Binance price, Polymarket bid, slug, side and
"price delta in bps".

**Model rows ("step3").** 357,390 rows over 2,251 markets (51% of 4,450). The coverage
audit: 1,241 with no Polymarket ticks, 922 with too few Binance trades, 21 missing
partitions, 14 with no valid book after filters, 1 tied BTC close (label undefined).

## Methods

**Clock validation.**
- Per-day minimum of ingest − source: Binance 99 ms, Polymarket 7 ms; the envelopes vary
  by ≤ 4 ms and ≤ 2 ms across days → relative drift bounded at **≤ 6 ms**.
- The constant offset is not identifiable from one vantage point: ambiguity **≈ ±99 ms**.
- "Synchronization-free" event study on the collector clock: Binance moves ≥ 5 bps
  within 1 s matched to the first directionally consistent UP-token best-bid change
  (≥ 1 tick) within 2 s.

**Stratifications.** Median lead–lag by |price_delta_bps| quintile and by disagreement
tercile (500k-pair subsample); Spearman of |lag| on |price_delta_bps|. Spread stylised
facts on a 200k-tick subsample.

**Forecasting.** 43 features: spread, best bid/ask, microprice, imbalance, depth, book
update velocity, returns, realised volatility, momentum, VWAP deviation, candle shape,
technical indicators.
- Walk-forward logistic regression by market, Platt scaling, 559 windows.
- Model and calibrator are fit only on pre-cutoff rows.
- Baseline: the Polymarket mid at the same feature cutoff.
- Metrics: AUC, Brier, ECE (10 equal-width bins), log loss.
- A paired market-block bootstrap (2,251 blocks, 1,000 resamples) is used for the
  diagnostic full-timeline comparison.

**Trading simulation.** Positive-EV entries with 1% fee and 0.5% slippage; a loss costs
one staked unit plus fee.

## Findings

**Synchronisation.**
- Lead–lag (n = 2,936,031): median **16 ms**, p5/p95 **−186 / 316 ms**.
- On 500k pairs: 67.6% tight, 27.0% medium, 5.5% wide.
- Of 15,148 large Binance moves, **4,272** matched to a Polymarket response; median
  response **347 ms** on the collector clock.
- Median lag stays at 16–19 ms across disagreement quintiles and 17–18 ms across
  terciles; Spearman ρ ≈ 0.00 between |lag| and |price_delta_bps| (500k pairs).

**Spreads (Table 3, 200k ticks).** Median 0.01, p90 0.01, p95 0.02, p99 0.03; **91.9%**
at one tick (≤ 0.011), 6.0% at two ticks.

**Forecasts (Table 2).**

| Model | Split | AUC | Brier | ECE | Log loss |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Naive mid prior | pooled OOS | **0.8405** | 0.163 | 0.014 | 0.485 |
| Logistic + Platt | pooled OOS | 0.8377 | 0.165 | 0.026 | 0.495 |
| Naive mid prior | diagnostic full | 0.8401 | 0.163 | 0.014 | 0.486 |
| Logistic + Platt | diagnostic full | 0.8415 | 0.163 | 0.027 | 0.489 |
| Drift (up-only) | diagnostic full | 0.7725 | 0.218 | 0.145 | 0.792 |
| Imbalance 60 s (sigmoid) | diagnostic full | 0.5863 | 0.246 | 0.031 | 0.685 |

- Pooled OOS (355,814 rows): the model does not beat the mid.
- Diagnostic full timeline: ΔAUC +0.0014, 95% CI [0.0005, 0.0023], one-sided p = 0.001,
  n = 357,390, but it includes training rows.
- Brier ≈ 0.162 at one-tick spreads vs ≈ 0.185 at ≥ 0.015; 0.159 in high-volatility vs
  0.164 in low-volatility terciles.
- Simulated trading: **−0.116** normalised payoff units per attempted trade.
- Runtime: step3 export ~63 s, training ~67 s on an Apple M5 Max.

## Reusable definitions

- Dual timestamps (`source_ts_ms`, `ingest_ts_ms`) per event; per-venue transport delay =
  ingest − source.
- Drift bound from the stability of per-day minimum-delay envelopes; single-vantage
  constant-offset ambiguity.
- Nearest-neighbour lag pairing within ±W with tight/medium/wide flags; the dedupe key
  for `lag_pairs_ms`.
- Collector-clock response lag after large external moves.
- "Mid prior" baseline sampled at the feature cutoff; walk-forward + Platt protocol;
  market-block bootstrap.
- Validation checklist: per-stream monotonic source timestamps, duplicate and reconnect
  counts, lag histograms by day and market, rebuild-and-checksum.

## Stated limitations

- BTC 15-minute markets only; may not transfer to political or long-dated markets.
- WebSocket gaps; ±99 ms constant-offset ambiguity; top-of-book backtests overstate
  execution; settlement labels can leak if mishandled; only 2,251 of 4,450 markets in
  step3; full features not on HF.
- Tree and neural models were exploratory and not benchmarked.
- Live trading adds latency, queue position and partial fills; no profitability claim.
- Future: other venues and assets, non-crypto markets, multi-vantage collection, richer
  book reconstruction, stronger baselines, a public leaderboard.

## Critical assessment

- **The 16 ms "lead–lag" is not a lead–lag measure.** Pairing each Polymarket tick with
  the nearest Binance tick in a symmetric window gives a median near zero whenever
  Binance ticks are dense, whatever the true dynamics. The ±99 ms offset ambiguity is
  also six times larger than the median. The authors partly concede this.
- **The 347 ms response is not free of transport delay.** On the collector clock, the
  measured lag = true lag + (Polymarket delay − Binance delay). With minimum delays of
  7 ms and 99 ms, the true response is probably about 90 ms longer. Only 28% of moves
  (4,272 / 15,148) found a matching response, and no placebo (random times) is given.
- **Labels and fees.** The paper never states which price defines the 15-minute
  outcome. A "tied BTC close" suggests labels are computed from a BTC price rather than
  read from Polymarket's resolution. To my knowledge these markets resolve from a
  Chainlink BTC/USD stream, not Binance; this needs checking. The flat 1% fee is not
  matched to the venue's actual fee schedule for these markets in 2026.
- **"price_delta_bps" is undefined.** It is computed between a BTC price and a
  Polymarket bid (a probability), so the disagreement stratification cannot be
  interpreted.
- **The forecast set-up pools all rows within a 15-minute window.** AUC 0.84 mixes rows
  near expiry (easy) with early rows. A null against the mid is credible; a per-horizon
  breakdown is missing.
- **Coverage is patchy** (54 of 93 days, 51% of markets), and selection into step3
  (Binance data present) is not random.
- **Trust:**
  - corpus counts and structure: **high** (manifested, reproducible, versioned);
  - one-tick spreads (91.9%): **high**;
  - the model does not beat the mid out of sample: **high** for this feature set;
  - Polymarket responds about 0.35 s after large Binance moves: **medium** (direction
    robust, size biased by transport delay, no placebo);
  - 16 ms median lead–lag and its stability across regimes: **low** (by construction,
    and an undefined disagreement metric);
  - −0.116 per trade simulated: **medium** (stylised fee and slippage).

## Directions it opens

- Join OpenMarket's millisecond books with on-chain fills for the same markets: who trades
  after Binance moves (wallet-level latency arbitrage), maker vs taker, and their PnL.
- Re-estimate the cross-venue response with proper event studies: Chainlink (resolution
  source) vs Binance vs Coinbase as the leader, a placebo, multiple vantage points, and
  Polymarket's own server timestamps.
- Per-horizon calibration of the mid within the 15-minute window (time-to-expiry ×
  moneyness surface), and a fair-value benchmark from a short-dated BTC option-style
  model.
- Extend the corpus to every crypto Up/Down series (5 m, 15 m, 1 h, daily; BTC/ETH/SOL/XRP)
  from the live WebSocket, keeping dual timestamps and the dedupe-key discipline.
- Fee-regime event studies on these markets (spread, depth, and trade count before/after
  fee changes).
- Product: a public, versioned Polymarket tick archive with source and ingest timestamps,
  gap reports and checksums; reuse the validation checklist for our own capture.
