# Polymarket-v1 Database

- **arXiv:** [2606.04217](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04217). v1 2 Jun 2026; v2 8 Jun 2026 (read).
  cs.CE, q-fin.ST, q-fin.TR. 35 pp, 17 figures, 15 tables, one category-mapping appendix.
- **Authors:** Boka Qin (Washington University in St. Louis) and Rui Yang (Southwest University
  of Political Science and Law). Both are affiliated with Time Seventeen, a trading firm.
- **Data:** HF [`TimeSeventeen/Polymarket-v1`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/TimeSeventeen/Polymarket-v1),
  CC-BY-4.0, about 49–53 GB Parquet. No code repository.
- **Read:** the full v2 PDF via text extraction, plus the HF dataset card. Figures were read from
  captions and axis text only. v1 was not compared.

## Question

This is primarily a **data release**. It covers the complete V1 CTF Exchange trade tape on
Polygon, with aggressor direction taken from the settlement layer. Three demonstration
questions:
- **(a)** How accurate are the tick rule and bulk volume classification (BVC) against true
  direction?
- **(b)** Do classification errors bias VPIN, order-flow imbalance (OFI) and transaction-cost
  analysis?
- **(c)** Does true microstructure quality predict forecast accuracy (Brier score), and do
  classified proxies attenuate the link?

The paper also reports stylized facts, a staggered-DiD of the 2026 fee rollout, Hasbrouck VAR
and VECM price discovery, an "informed trading efficacy" test, and a wash-trading proxy.

## Data

**Coverage.** 2022-11-21 → 2026-04-28 (41 months), V1 only. The paper calls 2026-04-28 the
"last settlement". The tape is built from `OrderFilled` events, with a single frozen metadata
snapshot joined on `asset_id`; the join succeeds for 99.8% of trades.

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Records, including "relayer" rows | 1.2016B |
| Nominal volume | $60.9B |
| "Economic" volume, relayer rows removed | $28.6B |
| Markets (condition_id) | 1,295,860 |
| Monthly Parquet partitions | 42 |

**The "relayer" filter.**
- Rows whose `taker` is `0x4bfb41d5…982e` or `0xc5d563a3…f80a` are removed. This is about 53% of
  nominal rows, or "50–55%" in Section 7.
- The paper calls these "platform-router addresses … for broker-routed flows".
- *Reviewer note:* these are the CTF Exchange and NegRisk CTF Exchange contracts. Akey et al.
  (SSRN 6443103, App. A.1) describe the rows where the exchange is the taker as the
  **taker-order leg of a matched trade, which double-counts it**.
- So "economic volume" is **de-duplicated fill volume**, not the removal of broker routing.
  This is consistent with the Paradigm/Slivkoff double-counting note the paper itself cites.

**Annual de-duplication (Table 14).**

| Year | Nominal | Economic | Nominal trades |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2023 | $0.07B | $0.04B | 0.33M |
| 2024 | $8.80B | $3.91B | 57.6M |
| 2025 | $20.97B | $9.69B | 241.2M |
| 2026 (to Apr 28) | $31.11B | $14.96B | 902.5M |

**Record structure.**
- Primary key in the paper is `(tx_hash, log_index)`. On HF, `id` is
  `chainId_blockNumber_logIndex`, with **no tx hash**.
- One taker transaction fans out to several maker rows. `block_timestamp` is in seconds.
- `price` is the traded token's price. `p_event` = price for `outcome_seq=1`, and 1 − price
  otherwise.
- `D = sign(taker_direction) × (+1 for seq 1 / −1 for seq 2)` puts every trade on the YES
  axis.
- In NegRisk markets, `p_event` is per condition and is not normalised across the group.

**HF layers.**
- `OrderFilled/`: raw, about 1.2B rows, 13 columns, including `maker_direction`,
  `taker_direction` and `fee_usdc`.
- `daily_aligned/`: about 602M rows. Standard binary, de-relayered, metadata joined, with
  `p_event` and `D`, plus `taker_base_fee`, `maker_base_fee`, `opens_at`, `close_at`,
  `resolved_at` and `winning_outcome_label`.
- `daily_aligned_multi/`: about 144M NegRisk rows.
- `CTF/`: about 839M rows of `preparations`, `splits`, `merges`, `resolutions` and
  `redemptions`. The card says this layer is partitioned by UTC+8 days, unlike the trade
  layers.

**Subsamples.**
- **"Standard Binary":** `neg_risk=false`, both legs trading, ≥30 trades after the filter.
  This is about 1,011,095 markets, roughly 285k fewer than the full set.
- **Classifier test:** 202M trades. Up-or-Down markets are excluded here, yet Up-Down appears
  as "Price Action" in Table 4.
- **VAR:** 6,223 markets (≥30 trades, ≥10 unique prices).
- **VECM:** 426 markets.
- **Brier panel:** 1,019 resolved markets, described as "balanced monthly observations".

**Categories.** Eight harmonised categories, keyword-based (Table 16), including "Price
Action" for Up-or-Down markets.

## Methods

- **Spreads:** Roll, Corwin-Schultz, Abdi-Ranaldo (said to be near-identical to Roll, so not
  shown), Gibbs effective spread (Hasbrouck 2009; Normal-Inverse-Gamma conjugate) on
  ∆p_t = c∆D_t + u_t.
- **Other liquidity and efficiency measures:** Amihud, Kyle λ, variance ratio VR(5).
- **Structural breaks:** Bai-Perron.
- **Order flow:** true OFI = ΣD·V/ΣV, and true VPIN versus BVC-VPIN.
- **Hasbrouck SVAR(5):** on x_t = D_t√V_t and ∆p_t, with Cholesky ordering (flow first).
  Permanent impact is the long-run response; transitory impact is contemporaneous minus
  permanent. PIR is permanent / total.
- **VECM between YES and NO legs:** Hasbrouck information share (upper bounds) and
  Gonzalo-Granger weights.
- **Classifier accuracy:** tick rule (zero-tick carries the last non-zero sign) and BVC versus
  `D`, in price bins of 0.01. Bias in OFI = (inferred − true)/|true|.
- **Direction autocorrelation:** ρ = Corr(D_t, D_{t−1}) by price bin.
- **Fee-reform DiD:** TWFE with market and month fixed effects. The treatment schedule is
  Crypto 2026-01, Sports ("selected leagues") 2026-02, everything else 2026-03.
  - There are three groups (Main, Sports, UpDown), plus event studies from k = −12 to +3.
  - Callaway-Sant'Anna and de Chaisemartin-D'Haultfœuille estimators are deferred.
- **"Informed trading efficacy":** P1 is a market's first large trade (≥$100 and above the
  market's 90th percentile). P2 is a run of ≥3 large same-direction trades. The hit rate is
  whether the trade's direction matches resolution, tested with a one-sided binomial.
- **Wash proxy:** directed maker→taker cycles of up to 5 hops within a market-month. Not
  validated.
- **Brier OLS:** Brier_m = mean_t (p_event,t − y_m)² across 1,019 markets.
  - Model 1: Gibbs and true VPIN. Model 2: Roll and BVC-VPIN.
  - Controls: Kyle λ, Amihud, mean price, trade count, category FE. The SE type is not stated.

**Longshot pattern (Table 3).** Return = payout − price, trade-weighted, both legs, resolved
markets only.

The sign matches the favorite–longshot bias, but magnitudes are under 1¢ per share.

| Side | Addresses | Top 1% share | Gini |

True VPIN is undefined in 5 of 8 categories, and the median wash share is 0.0000 everywhere.

**Kyle λ.** The time series is predominantly **negative**, which the authors attribute to
"resolution pressure". The positive medians in the tables use absolute values.

- Sports VPIN +0.042; UpDown VPIN +0.095.
- The authors themselves call t > 100 implausible. VPIN and Amihud show pre-trends.
- Story offered: a taker fee drives out noise traders, flow becomes more toxic, spreads widen.
  Fees also curb wash trading.

**Hasbrouck VAR, 6,223 markets.**
- Median |permanent impact| 0.00152; median |transitory impact| 0.00177.
- Median PIR 0.455 (IQR 0.245–0.687).
- The mean is about 45× the median because of divergent fits in thin markets.

**VECM, 426 markets (6.9% of the VAR set).** Median GG weight on YES is 0.501 (IQR
0.480–0.523), so the two legs contribute symmetrically.

**Classifiers, 202M trades.**
- Overall accuracy: tick rule 49.83%, BVC 50.51%.
- The tick rule is about 52–54% in bins below 0.10, falls monotonically, and reaches about
  44–46% in bins 0.93–0.98.
- An earlier draft reported 69.3% on a liquid March-2024 subsample; it is now called a
  selection artifact.
- Median OFI bias (178,643 market-months): tick rule +0.18, BVC −1.36. Median direction
  error: +0.038 and −0.300.
- Direction autocorrelation is strongly positive for prices between 0.2 and 0.8. No numbers
  are given; it is shown in a figure only.

**Brier regression (n = 1,019).**

| Regressor | Model 1 (true measures) | Model 2 (classified) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spread | Gibbs −4.128 (SE 0.774) | Roll −1.304 (SE 0.307) |
| VPIN | True 0.198 (SE 0.037, t = 5.34) | BVC 0.183 (SE 0.056, t = 3.29) |

- Kyle λ, Amihud, mean price and trade count are not significant.
- The authors read "wider spread → lower Brier" as a selection effect: informed specialists
  in niche markets.

**Trends.**
- Activity peaks at the 2024 election and decays as liquidity migrates to V2.
- The maker Gini rises after the February 2026 maker-rebate launch.

## Reusable definitions

- The HF schema: `p_event`, `D`, the `daily_aligned` layer, and Standard Binary
  (neg_risk = false, both legs trading, ≥30 trades).
- The two exchange addresses to drop or re-key, which de-duplicates `OrderFilled`.
- True VPIN and OFI computed with D.
- Hasbrouck PIR on trade time.
- The 8-category keyword map (Table 16).
- The TVL proxy: 30-day rolling USDC volume in markets unresolved at trade time.

## Stated limitations (and future work)

1. Settlement layer only; no order book.
2. Offshore V1 only.
3. Unclear validity for V2.
4. Some markets lack winner labels.
5. Standard, Up-Down and NegRisk must not be pooled.
6. No oracle alignment or continuous updates (unlike Jia et al. 2604.20421).
7. Sports is mostly excluded from Table 4.
8. VPIN and Amihud DiD estimates have pre-trends.
9. The wash proxy is unvalidated.

Mint/burn decomposition (PositionSplit/Merge) is said to be needed but is not done, even
though the `CTF/` layer ships those logs. Heterogeneity-robust DiD is left for later.

## Critical assessment

**Mislabelled de-duplication.** "Relayer/broker-routed flow" is really the exchange-as-taker
leg. The conclusion (drop the rows) is right, but readers may misinterpret "economic volume".

**Classifier result looks like a normalisation artifact.**
- Accuracy runs from about 53% to 45% as price rises, mirror-symmetric around 0.5. That is
  the pattern a YES/NO leg-mixing or sign-flip effect would produce.
- The robustness check (outcome_seq = 1 only) is asserted in one sentence with no numbers.
- Near-coin-flip tick-rule accuracy on a tape with strong same-direction runs is surprising;
  runs should *help* a tick rule unless prices are reconstructed across legs.

**Look-ahead in the "informed trading" test.** The hit rate ignores price. Buying at 0.9 and
winning is a "hit", so 52.3% versus 50.2% says little about edge. The P1 definition (above
the market's 90th percentile of size) also uses the whole market's trade distribution.

**Brier regression is weak.**
- VPIN is missing for 5 of 8 categories, yet n = 1,019 is called balanced.
- Brier is averaged over a market's life, so markets that are long-lived or near 0.5
  mechanically score worse.
- The spread "selection" story is post hoc. Two estimators change at once, as the authors
  admit.

**DiD is not credible.**
- The treatment timing conflicts with Akey et al.: crypto 1h/4h/daily/weekly from
  2026-03-06, NCAA and Serie A from 2026-02-18, all markets from 2026-03-30.
- It also conflicts with the Akey HF card's "Q4 2024 fee introduction".
- The t-stats are implausible, there are pre-trends, and V2 migration is a confounder.
- Use the per-market `taker_base_fee` and `fee_usdc` fields, not a category calendar.

**Smaller inconsistencies.**
- "$0.07B economic 2023" in the text versus $0.04B in Table 14.
- Table 2 says Akey et al. cover 2021–2024; they cover 2022-11 to 2026-03.
- Median wash share of 0 everywhere, yet the time series is said to vary.
- Kyle λ is signed in one place and absolute in another.

**Trust.**

| Claim | Trust | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tape coverage, counts, schema | **High** | Public, reproducible |
| De-duplication magnitude (~47% of USDC) | **High** | Matches the double-count mechanics |
| Classifier near-random + gradient | **Medium-low** | Possible normalisation artifact; check independently |
| VPIN/OFI distortion | **Medium** | Follows if the classifier result holds |
| Fee DiD | **Low** | Wrong or contested timing, pre-trends, inflated t |
| Brier ↔ microstructure | **Low** | Small, selected panel; missing regressor |
| VAR PIR ≈ 0.45 | **Medium** | Thin-market fits, median only |
| Longshot sign | **Medium-high** | Trade-weighted and tiny |
| Maker concentration | **High** | Simple and robust |

## Directions it opens

- **Rebuild the classifier benchmark** on our own V1+V2 fills. Run the tick rule within each
  token, on the YES axis, and per match (orderHash). Settle whether the 50% figure is real
  and publish a direction-labelled benchmark.
- **Use the shipped `CTF/` logs** (splits, merges, redemptions) to label every fill as
  mint/merge/transfer and to build complete-set-aware volume. Reconcile with Akey's 588M trades
  and GC's $13.76B for 2023–2025, which ≈ this paper's 2023–25 economic USDC of $13.64B.
- **Fee event study done properly:** per-market fee fields, the exact activation timestamps
  in our metadata, Callaway-Sant'Anna, and V2 as a separate regime.
- **A V1↔V2 continuity table:** the same market keys across exchanges, the migration date,
  and liquidity migration curves.
- **Market-level Hasbrouck/PIR and true-VPIN panels as a public product,** with
  category- and price-normalized spreads (ticks).
- **Brier versus microstructure redone** with time-sampled prices, horizon controls and the
  full resolved universe. Also add depth from live WebSocket data, which V1 lacks.
- **A wash-cycle detector compared with other measures,** using HHI, Sirolly's network score
  and flip pairs on the same tape.
- **Direction-run (autocorrelation) statistics** per market as a toxicity or momentum signal,
  with tests of whether runs precede price moves.
